THE UNTOLD STORY:

Set in the vibrant Land of Oz, Wicked reveals the untold story of the Witches of Oz. At Shiz University, an unexpected friendship forms between the misunderstood, green-skinned Elphaba and the exceptionally popular Glinda. As extraordinary forces, political intrigue, and personal choices pull them apart, their bonds are tested, changing their lives and the fate of Oz, forever.
